Officine Gặp is a curatorial, spatial and research practice dedicated to articulating alternative ways of reading and engaging with place and processes of urban becoming.

Through fieldwork, artistic production, conversation, curation, publishing and collaboration, the practice brings people, places, stories and forms of knowledge into relation, generating new encounters and ways of understanding place.
