Our approach
The Approach — 02

Care is a cultural act.

Gặp Art Services is the delivery arm of Officine Gặp, a research-led cultural practice founded in Sài Gòn. We produce exhibitions and cultural programs with Italian production discipline and museum-standard care — through the local networks, languages and craft of a practice rooted in Việt Nam.

How an engagement runs

Consult & plan

Feasibility, budgets, schedules and method statements — the whole project planned on paper before anything is built or moved.

Produce & build

Fabrication, graphics, sound and light, artists and venues — the exhibition produced and managed as one piece of work.

Install & care

Artworks installed to conservation standards, documented at every step — then struck, wrapped and cared for after the opening.

We work with

Museums & institutions · Galleries & art fairs · Biennials & festivals · Architects & developers · Private collectors · Artists & estates
